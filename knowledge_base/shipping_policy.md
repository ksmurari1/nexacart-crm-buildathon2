# NexaCart Shipping & Delivery Policy

## Processing time
Orders placed before 2:00 PM (local warehouse time) on a business day are packed and handed to the courier the same day. Orders placed after 2:00 PM, on weekends or on public holidays are dispatched the next business day.

## Delivery timelines
- Standard Shipping: 3-5 business days after dispatch. Free on orders over $50, otherwise $4.99.
- Express Shipping: 1-2 business days after dispatch. $12.99 flat fee.
- Same-Day Delivery: available in selected metro areas for orders placed before 11:00 AM. $19.99.
Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays.

## Tracking your order
A tracking link is emailed as soon as your order is dispatched. You can also see live tracking under My Account > Orders > Track Package. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first scan after dispatch.

## Late or delayed orders
If your order has not arrived by the end of the estimated delivery date:
1. Check the tracking page for courier updates or a "delivery attempted" notice.
2. Check with neighbours, building reception or your parcel locker.
3. Wait one additional business day - most late parcels arrive within 24 hours of the estimate.
If the package is still not delivered 2 business days after the estimated date, contact support with your order ID. We will open a trace with the courier within 1 business day and update you within 48 hours.

## Express shipping guarantee
If an Express Shipping order arrives late because of a NexaCart or courier delay, the $12.99 express fee is refunded to the original payment method once the delay is confirmed by the courier trace. Delays caused by an incorrect address or by severe weather are excluded.

## Lost or stolen packages
If the courier trace confirms a package is lost, or tracking shows "Delivered" but you did not receive it and the courier cannot locate it within 5 business days, we will send a free replacement or issue a full refund - your choice.

## Changing the delivery address
The delivery address can be changed from My Account > Orders until the order status is "Dispatched". After dispatch, address changes are not possible; you may refuse delivery and the item will be returned to us for a full refund once received.

## International shipping
We ship to 32 countries. International delivery takes 7-14 business days. Import duties and taxes are payable by the recipient unless stated as "Duties Included" at checkout.
