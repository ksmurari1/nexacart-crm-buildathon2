# NexaCart Returns & Refund Policy

## Return window
Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be unused, in their original packaging and with all tags attached. Footwear must be tried on indoors only and returned with the original shoe box.

## Non-returnable items
Gift cards, personalised or engraved items, opened personal-care products, underwear and swimwear (hygiene seal removed), and perishable food cannot be returned unless they arrived damaged or defective.

## How to start a return
1. Go to My Account > Orders and select the order.
2. Click "Return or Exchange" and choose the item(s) and reason.
3. Print the prepaid return label (free for returns within 30 days) or choose a drop-off QR code.
4. Drop the parcel at any partner courier point within 7 days of creating the return.
You will receive an email confirmation when the return is created and again when it is received at our warehouse.

## Refund timelines
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 3 business days after the returned item is received and inspected at our warehouse. Your bank may take an additional 5-10 business days to show the credit. Original shipping fees are refunded only if the item was damaged, defective or sent in error.

## Exchanges and wrong size
Size and colour exchanges are free within 30 days. Start an exchange from My Account > Orders > Return or Exchange and choose "Exchange". The replacement ships as soon as the courier scans your return parcel. If the new size is out of stock you will receive a full refund instead.

## Wrong item received
If you received an item different from what you ordered, report it within 14 days of delivery. We send the correct item at no cost and provide a prepaid label to return the wrong one.

## Late returns
Returns received between 31 and 45 days after delivery are accepted for store credit only. Returns after 45 days cannot be accepted.
