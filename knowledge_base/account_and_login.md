# NexaCart Account & Login Help

## Resetting your password
1. Open the NexaCart sign-in page and click "Forgot password?".
2. Enter the email address registered with your account.
3. Open the reset email from no-reply@nexacart.com and click "Reset password". The link is valid for 30 minutes and can be used only once.
4. Choose a new password with at least 8 characters, including one number and one symbol.
After a successful reset you are signed out on all other devices for your security.

## Password reset email not received
- Allow up to 10 minutes for the email to arrive.
- Check your Spam, Junk and Promotions folders.
- Add no-reply@nexacart.com to your safe-senders list.
- Make sure you entered the email address used at sign-up; we cannot send reset links to unregistered addresses.
- You can request a new link after 2 minutes; requesting a new link invalidates the previous one.
If you still do not receive the email after these steps, contact support and we will verify your identity and reset the password manually.

## Account locked after failed attempts
For security, an account is locked for 30 minutes after 5 failed sign-in attempts. You can wait 30 minutes or unlock it immediately with "Forgot password?".

## Two-step verification (OTP)
One-time passcodes are sent by SMS or authenticator app and expire after 5 minutes. If you changed your phone number and cannot receive codes, use one of the 10 backup codes shown when two-step verification was enabled, or contact support for identity verification.

## Changing your email address
Go to My Account > Profile > Email, enter the new address and confirm it from the verification email. Your order history stays linked to your account.

## Suspicious activity
If you see orders or changes you did not make, reset your password immediately and contact support. Our Trust & Safety team reviews compromised-account reports and can freeze the account to prevent further orders.

## Deleting your account
Account deletion can be requested from My Account > Privacy. Deletion is completed within 30 days; open orders and refunds must be completed first.
