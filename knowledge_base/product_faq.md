# NexaCart Product FAQ

## Damaged items on arrival
If an item arrives damaged, broken or with missing parts, report it within 7 days of delivery from My Account > Orders > Report a Problem and upload one or two photos of the item and packaging. Once the photos are reviewed (usually within 1 business day) you can choose a free replacement or a full refund including shipping fees. You do not need to return the damaged item unless we send you a prepaid label.

## Defective products and warranty
All electrical and kitchen appliances sold by NexaCart include a 12-month manufacturer warranty from the delivery date. If an appliance stops working within 30 days, we replace it directly. Between 30 days and 12 months, warranty claims are handled through the manufacturer; we provide the invoice and the manufacturer's service contact on request.

## Product sizing
Size charts are available on every clothing and footwear product page. If you are between two sizes we recommend choosing the larger size. Free size exchanges are available within 30 days.

## Stock and restock alerts
Out-of-stock products show a "Notify me" button. You will receive an email when the item is back in stock. We cannot reserve items or guarantee restock dates.

## Gift cards
NexaCart digital gift cards are available in values from $10 to $500 and are delivered by email within 15 minutes. Gift cards never expire, cannot be exchanged for cash and cannot be used to buy other gift cards.

## Price match
We do not offer price matching with other retailers. If the price of an item you bought drops on NexaCart within 7 days of purchase, contact support for a one-time store-credit adjustment of the difference.

## Contacting support and escalation
Support is available by chat and email 24/7. Standard tickets receive a first reply within 24 hours. Complex, urgent or sensitive tickets are escalated to a senior support specialist, who contacts the customer within 4 business hours.
