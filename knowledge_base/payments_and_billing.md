# NexaCart Payments & Billing Policy

## Accepted payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay and NexaCart gift cards. Cash on delivery is not available.

## Pending authorisations
When you place an order, your bank may place a temporary authorisation hold. If an order is cancelled or a payment attempt fails, the hold is released automatically by your bank, usually within 3-7 business days. A hold is not a completed charge.

## Duplicate or multiple charges
If you see more than one charge for the same order, first check whether the extra entries are pending authorisations (see above). If two or more charges are marked as completed for a single order, contact support with your order ID and the last 4 digits of the card. Confirmed duplicate charges are reversed within 5 business days after verification by our Billing team.

## Unauthorised charges
If you do not recognise a NexaCart charge, contact support immediately. Our Trust & Safety team investigates every unauthorised-charge report and may freeze the related account while the case is open. Do not share your full card number by chat or email.

## Promo codes and discounts
Only one promo code can be used per order. Codes are case-sensitive, have an expiry date and may exclude sale items, gift cards and some brands. A promo code cannot be applied after the order has been placed.

## Invoices
A VAT/GST invoice is available under My Account > Orders > Download Invoice once the order is dispatched.
